Important labels

F: Principal focus.

2F: Twice the focal length from a lens.

C: Centre of curvature of a spherical mirror (at 2F).

P: Pole, the middle of a mirror.

What does “behind the mirror” mean?
The object is in front of the mirror. Behind means the other side of the mirror, where the image appears to be. Light does not actually meet there for a virtual image.
